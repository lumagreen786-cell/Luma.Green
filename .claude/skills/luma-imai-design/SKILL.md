# Luma Imai Design

Use the current Luma UI contract in docs/design/designer-system.md. Choose the task and primary action before the layout. Use Astryx components with the original Luma light/dark colours, Geist/Noto and Lucide. The owner explicitly retained the original palette on 16 October 2026. Use aligned reading grids, open sections and dividers. Avoid card nesting and filler metrics.

Use large recycling photography on public editorial pages and role selection. Keep login, signup and collection forms centred without a side panel. On phones, keep the primary action easy to reach. Operational records need clear data, not decorative art. Reserve space for loading and errors without hiding their text. Check light/dark, phone/tablet/desktop and translated scripts including RTL. Preserve entered values and focus across errors.

Source: `.agents/skills/imai-design-system/SKILL.md` and its surfaces, feedback and visual-verification rules in imai-dev. IMAI chat-first layouts, avatars and exact density settings are not Luma requirements.
