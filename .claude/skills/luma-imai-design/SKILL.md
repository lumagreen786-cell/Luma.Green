# Luma Imai Design

Use the current Luma UI contract in docs/design/designer-system.md. Choose the task and primary action before the layout. Keep neutral surfaces, green actions, existing corner tokens, Geist/Noto and Lucide. Use aligned reading grids, open sections and dividers. Avoid card nesting and filler metrics.

Give public entry pages an editorial recycling image beside the useful action. On phones, keep imagery compact so the form remains easy to reach. Operational records need clear data, not decorative art. Reserve space for loading and errors without hiding their text. Check light/dark, phone/tablet/desktop and translated scripts including RTL. Preserve entered values and focus across errors.

Source: `.agents/skills/imai-design-system/SKILL.md` and its surfaces, feedback and visual-verification rules in imai-dev. IMAI chat-first layouts, avatars and exact density settings are not Luma requirements.
