## Problems in the supplied sample

1. P112 uses `https://googleapis.com{GOOGLE_API_KEY}` as a placeholder URL. It is not a working Travel Impact Model endpoint. Its failure path returns zero, which falsely looks like zero emissions.
2. P21 gives 0.001–0.003 kg per litre, then calls this approximately 10.4 kg per 1,000 litres. The first range equals 1–3 kg per 1,000 litres. These numbers conflict.
3. Table1 models a cyclic fridge at 2 kWh/day. A simple 200 W × 24 h calculation gives 4.8 kWh/day. Rated power and compressor duty cycle must be distinct inputs.
4. P149 spreads flight emissions over 365 days, while nearby prose describes a daily total without a clear allocation period. Show trip emissions separately; disclose any annual allocation.
5. The sample claims five pillars but supplies no complete food/consumption engine. It contains no persistence and no production authorization layer.
6. P344–351 and P578–582 are mock BBPS replies. Never copy these into a live path or present them as bill-provider responses.
7. P414–418 does not match its own tariff math. With its stated formula, ₹1850 / 1.09 − ₹110 is about ₹1587.25, and its slabs give about 229.14 kWh, not 235.27 kWh. This check validates only the internal arithmetic, not whether the tariff is official/current.
8. The regional tariff algorithm includes a zero-price TNEB slab. Boundary handling must avoid division by zero, and free units/subsidies require eligibility rules. No undocumented rate is safe to present as a current official tariff.
9. Greywater reuse and rooftop solar can yield modeled avoided emissions. They do not create issued carbon credits or certified offsets.

## Safe implementation slices

1. **Daily household records:** add authenticated owner-only daily logs, explicit period/members and measured electricity first. Version each factor/source. Add bounded input validation, exact units, audit events, edit history and deletion/export rules. Do not label incomplete pillars as a complete footprint.
2. **Manual five-pillar calculator:** specify separate housing, utilities, transport, food and consumption contracts. Source and date each factor before enabling a category. Include appliance duty cycles and prevent double counting meter electricity against appliance estimates. Keep measured, estimated, avoided and issued values separate.
3. **Household journey gaps:** add user-selected discovery sorting, supported-role eligibility and explicit apartment/independent-home routing. Add operator evidence uploads to private R2 and test owner/operator/denied reads. Apply Florin compositions, all locales and both themes.
4. **Provider integrations:** connect Google TIM, BBPS and gold/coupon settlement only after exact provider, account scope, commercial terms, allowance, privacy and live acceptance are established. Honest unavailable states can ship before execution is enabled.
5. **Vehicle events:** define task-scoped foreground location, retention, stop rules, recipient consent and reliable ETA events. Do not start Wi-Fi scanning/background employee tracking as a side effect of the general document request. Keep proximity separate from proof of handover or payment.

PostHog/Sentry review: analytics must never include exact coordinates, family phones, consumer numbers, bills, photos or daily private lifestyle records. Product events can record only approved action/status categories with consent. Provider errors need shared redaction; the source samples' raw exception/zero-fallback handling is not suitable for Production.
