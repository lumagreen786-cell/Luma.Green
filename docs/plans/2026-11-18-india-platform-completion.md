## What complete means

Use a finite, versioned reference universe. The national industry baseline is
MoSPI NIC 2025. Its official release reports 22 sections, 87 divisions, 257
groups, 463 classes and 1,887 six-digit subclasses. Verify the downloaded
official structure against these counts; preserve discrepancies rather than
inventing rows. Record the exact source URL, retrieval date, hash, version and
source row for imported definitions. Keep NIC 2008 identifiers and workbook
sector identifiers distinct; a four-digit concordance does not prove an exact
subclass match.

Source: [MoSPI release, 18 November 2025](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2191142).

The current geography owner already lists 36 states and Union Territories.
Test all of them. Do not describe a state selector as a complete state-specific
legal or service integration. A service requires an eligible operator and
current facility scope in the requested locality.

Maintain three separate denominators:

1. **Reference coverage:** every official activity, state/UT and supplied source
   item is mapped, or has an explicit unresolved source reason.
2. **Functional coverage:** each requirement and applicable workflow family has
   a usable screen, enforced backend contract and passing acceptance scenario.
   A catalogue row alone earns no functional completion.
3. **External acceptance:** actual provider delivery, reviewed facility evidence,
   official system access and device checks. Configuration and test fixtures
   alone earn no live acceptance.

An activity may generate several material streams. Select actual material,
process, site and handling needs; do not infer hazard, price, recipe, permitted
receiver or credit entitlement from an industry code. A controlled or excluded
activity must have an explicit review/unavailable route, not an ordinary
marketplace shortcut. A correctly blocked route can satisfy a software
requirement; it cannot be reported as accepted regulated dispatch.

The six original documents and six printed photos are not present at their
original paths. Attributed repository extracts remain usable with their source
labels. Original byte-level reconciliation remains open. Ignore handwriting.

## Architecture and minimum change

Keep one material catalogue, inventory ledger, lot lineage graph, custody
workflow, commercial order system and claim allocation owner. Industry and
jurisdiction references configure those owners; they do not introduce parallel
ledgers or grant account permissions.

Code is required because current participant decisions do not activate
operational capabilities, processing evidence does not post authoritative
stock, and ordinary trade stops at four sequential organisation tiers.
Documentation or a larger catalogue cannot fix those behaviors.

Reuse current facility declarations and reviewed scopes, material eligibility,
stock reservations, immutable inspections and corrections, draft billing,
household receipts, saved electricity logs, family grants and apartment routes.
Do not rebuild these working features from stale delivery notes.

TDD Route: auto / strict for changed permissions, stock, order, claim and schema
behavior under repository test rules. Reproduce relevant failures before
implementation and keep target regression tests. Source imports use provenance,
hierarchy, uniqueness and count checks. Low-impact prose uses formatting and
readback checks.
