# Ship X — Luma design inspiration

Reviewed: 11 November 2026.

## Owner direction

The owner rejected the earlier six concepts and the later sidebar workbench.
They selected Ship X as the main inspiration for Luma's page layouts, graphs,
controls and overall style. After initially rejecting its palette, the owner
supplied the Ship X brand screenshot and explicitly selected these five colours:
`#EBFF00`, `#A8E665`, `#317F38`, `#144419`, `#FFFFFF`.
Use only these base colours; translucent mixes with white may form quiet surfaces
and separators. The owner also requested removal of dark mode. The new design
is light-only. This decision supersedes earlier palette exploration and the old
dual-theme design direction. Live-app theme removal belongs to the subsequent
implementation, not the isolated sample. Do not claim it is already removed.

Prepare several connected Luma page samples before the platform-wide rollout.
Existing Luma photos may be used. Keep real workflows, access rules, exact units,
translations and required warnings when implementing the approved design.

## Carry into the Luma samples

- Horizontal navigation with a clear selected state, rather than the rejected sidebar.
- Soft page background; compact, rounded content surfaces with restrained borders.
- Clear page title and a single primary action.
- Graphs with a thin line, diagonal hatch fill, sparse grid and a precise selected-value label.
- Material breakdowns with rounded horizontal bars, direct labels and exact units.
- Tables and status filters near the task; detail views with short timelines.
- Consistent type, control height, spacing and corner shape across pages.
- Photo context drawn from Luma's own library, clearly distinct from record evidence.

## Adaptation boundaries

Do not copy Ship X logos, brand art, marketing text, fabricated logistics values,
maps or customer identities. Mock data must stay visibly labelled and isolated.
Do not imply live tracking, credit issuance, provider execution or payments.
Households do not estimate material weights. Phone verification stays required.
Charts need accessible text/data equivalents. Final fonts need Luma's full script
coverage. Native distribution remains outside this visual exploration.

The requested five samples cover Dashboard, Collections, Collection Detail,
Materials and Household Recycling. Each has its own local review tab.
These are design proposals, not a completed app rollout. Palette and
light-only direction are approved; final page approval remains open.

## Sample review checkpoint

Five light-only sample pages were opened in separate browser tabs. Desktop
captures of all five pages were inspected. The graph period switch changed the
sample total from 146 kg over seven days to 77 kg over three days. The collection
filter reduced six rows to the two awaiting assignment. The household form
advanced through its three stages and retained the sample area in review; its
finish action explicitly confirmed that nothing was submitted.

This is an English visual prototype. It does not establish production, provider,
authentication, mobile-device or full locale acceptance. Live application code,
theme settings and records were not changed. Dark-mode removal remains part of
the subsequent implementation. No deployment or native build was made.
